Tokens:Token is a small unit of the code

Tokens contains 
1.identifer
2.operator
3.literal
4.keywords
5.punctuations

1.Identifier: Identifier is name assign to the variable or function 
rules:
1.contains uppercase lowercase digits _
2.no special char
3.Identifers not start with digits and 
4.keywords are not allowed as Identifers
5.it is a case sensitive language 

2.operators:operator or nothing performing a mathematical operation on the values

3.literals:literals are nothing but values

4.keywords:keywords are predefined words or reserved words

5.punctuation:are used in indentation
